agent receipts / proof of concept

Prove what your agent did.

Every step an agent takes gets sealed into a hash chain. Change one character of history and verification catches exactly where. Run an episode, tamper with it, replay it.

Honest boundary: an unkeyed hash chain is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof. Anyone holding the receipts can rewrite the history and re-seal it, and a receipt does not prove the step actually happened. Real proof needs the seal anchored somewhere trusted, such as a signature, a timestamp or a public post. This demo shows detection, not anchoring.

What this is

Agents take actions: searching, calculating, sending. A receipt chain makes that history tamper-evident. Each receipt hashes the step's tool, input, output and the previous receipt's hash, so editing any step breaks the seal at that step and invalidates everything after it.

Replayable tools (fixed data, pure functions) are re-executed during replay and compared with the recorded output. Side-effecting steps like send_summary can't be honestly re-run; their receipt is the record. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data leaves this page.

Built as a public proof of concept for agent reliability work. MIT licensed.
